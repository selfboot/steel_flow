# SteelFlow: Metal Calculator

SteelFlow turns dimensions into a clear, verifiable metal estimate—and turns that estimate into a client-ready quote.

CALCULATE METAL WEIGHT

Choose a profile, enter dimensions and quantity, and see unit weight, total weight, surface area, volume, and the formula behind the result. SteelFlow supports common profiles including plate and flat bar, round and square bar, hex bar, round, square and rectangular tube, angle, channel, I/H sections, C/Z sections, and rebar.

ESTIMATE REAL PROJECT COST

Price material by kg, lb, meter, foot, or piece. Add waste, processing charges, transport or other fixed fees, markup or margin, and tax. Currency-aware rounding keeps totals practical for real quotes.

BUILD PROJECTS, NOT LOOSE CALCULATIONS

Save multiple material items into a project, duplicate and reorder them, update pricing, and keep every dimension and assumption traceable. See total quantity, weight, material cost, fees, tax, and quote total in one place.

SHARE PROFESSIONAL OUTPUT

Create a polished PDF quote or export a machine-friendly CSV material list. Add company and customer details, terms, validity, and notes before sharing.

MATERIALS AND UNITS THAT FIT YOUR WORK

Use built-in densities for carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum, copper, brass, titanium and more, or create custom materials. Work in metric or imperial units without rebuilding a calculation.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE

No account is required. Project and material data stays on your device by default, core calculations work offline, and versioned local backup helps you move or restore your records.

FREE TO START, ONE-TIME PRO UPGRADE

Quick calculations and essential profiles are free. SteelFlow Pro unlocks unlimited projects and items, custom quote branding and terms, complete PDF/CSV export, backup, batch tools, and advanced profiles with one non-consumable purchase—no subscription.

IMPORTANT

Weights and prices are theoretical estimates based on your inputs, reference densities, and common engineering formulas. Verify material grades, current standards, supplier data, drawings, and actual stock before purchasing, fabrication, transport planning, structural design, settlement, or contract use.
