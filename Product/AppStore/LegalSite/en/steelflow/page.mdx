# SteelFlow

SteelFlow is an offline-first iPhone and iPad app for metal-weight calculations, fabrication estimates, and client-ready quotes.

- Calculate plate, bar, tube, angle, channel, I/H section, and custom-section weight.
- Use metric or imperial dimensions with built-in or custom material densities.
- Track material prices, waste, processing fees, markup or margin, and tax.
- Organize estimates into projects and export PDF quotes or CSV material lists.
- Back up and restore your local SteelFlow data without creating an account.

Project, supplier, company, and quote data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share a file.
