# Metal weight to a customer quote

Calculate steel, aluminium and other metal weights, turn your own material prices into an estimate, and share a PDF quote from your iPhone or iPad. Work offline without creating an account.

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id6806282417" style={{display:'inline-block',padding:'14px 22px',background:'#125782',color:'#fff',borderRadius:'12px',fontWeight:700,margin:'12px 0'}}>Download SteelFlow on the App Store</a>

Free to download. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, with no subscription. The App Store shows your local price.

## From dimensions to a quote in three steps

1. **Calculate weight.** Choose a plate, bar, pipe, tube or structural section. Enter dimensions in metric or imperial units and use a built-in or custom material density.
2. **Build your estimate.** Save the calculation to a project. Enter your material price, waste, processing fees, markup or margin, and tax. Add the client and check the totals.
3. **Preview and share.** Choose a PDF style and A4 or Letter paper. Share the free PDF with SteelFlow branding, or use Pro for your company details and logo without SteelFlow branding.

## Built for workshop and purchasing work

Estimate plates, round and square bars, pipes, rectangular tubes, angles, channels, I/H sections and custom areas. Reuse your material prices and project items instead of rebuilding every estimate. App languages include English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, Spanish, Mexican Spanish and French.

Project, supplier, client and quote content stays on your device unless you export or share it. RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase and entitlement information. See the [privacy policy](./privacy) for details.
